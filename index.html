About

I work where design and code meet. I like blocking out a space, finding out what the player needs from it, then building the systems that make it work: objectives, puzzles, interactables, enemy behaviour and the tools designers use to tune them.

Before games I spent 14 years in project management, QA and team leadership, so I'm at home with requirements, deadlines and working across disciplines.
