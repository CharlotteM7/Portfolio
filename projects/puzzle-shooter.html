Level & Combat Design · Solo

Puzzle-Action Shooter

  • Role: Solo designer and developer
  • Context: Prototype
  • Engine: Unreal Engine 5
  • Tech: Blueprints, Behavior Trees, AI Perception, NavMesh
  • Platform: PC

Overview

A third-person prototype that alternates combat with environmental puzzles. The challenge was keeping puzzles readable without losing combat tension, so I sequenced fights and puzzle gates as separate beats and leaned on clear UI feedback. Enemies use Behavior Trees and perception to hunt the player across the navmesh, and an ammo economy pushes players to engage with the puzzle routes.

My role

  • Designed the level layout and encounter pacing.
  • Built player movement, combat, ammo, damage and UI feedback in Blueprints.
  • Set up enemy AI with Behavior Trees, AI Perception and NavMesh movement.
  • Scripted puzzle triggers and interactables into the encounter flow.
