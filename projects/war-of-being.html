Overview

War of Being is a sci-fi adventure in UE5 development at DMTesseracT. An early beta came out on Steam years ago, and the game has changed a lot since. I'm designing levels for the version in development now. I take areas from layout and blockout through to scripted sequences, working with the rest of the team on how each space plays. The project is under NDA, so I can't share screenshots or maps here.

What I own

  • Design and greybox new level spaces using UE5 blockout workflows.
  • Program interactable objects, triggers, narrative dialogue trees and scripted Level Sequences in Blueprints.
  • Shape encounter flow, pacing and player-readable navigation.
  • Iterate on layouts through internal playtesting.
  • Collaborate in a small development team using Perforce.
