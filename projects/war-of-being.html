The game

War of Being is a first-person narrative RPG built around War of Being, the concept album by the band Tesseract. Players live inside the story the album tells: a journey through fractured identity, inner conflict and what it means to exist. The music isn't just a soundtrack; it shapes how the world is built.

It's in pre-alpha in Unreal Engine 5 for PC (Steam). An early beta came out on Steam years ago, and the game has changed a lot since.

My part

I'm one of the game developers on the studio's small core team. I take areas from layout and blockout through to scripted gameplay and sequences, turning the album's story beats into spaces players can explore. The project is under NDA, so I can't share screenshots or maps of my levels here.

What I own

  • Design and greybox new level spaces using UE5 blockout workflows.
  • Program interactable objects, triggers, narrative dialogue trees and scripted Level Sequences in Blueprints.
  • Shape encounter flow, pacing and player-readable navigation.
  • Iterate on layouts through internal playtesting.
  • Collaborate in a small development team using Perforce.
