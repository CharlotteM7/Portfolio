Overview

War of Being is a sci-fi VR game in Early Access on Steam. I'm designing levels that will ship as new content in an upcoming update. I take areas from layout and blockout through to scripted sequences, working with the rest of the team on how each space plays in VR. The project is under NDA, so I can't share screenshots or maps here.

What I own

  • Level design for new gameplay areas in an upcoming content update.
  • Level sequences that carry story beats and pacing.
  • Blueprint logic for gameplay systems and level scripting.
  • Design notes and documentation, updated after team reviews and playtests.
